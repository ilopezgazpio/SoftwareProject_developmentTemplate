## 2. Team diagnosis

We cover ideas (Plant), coordination (Co-ordinator), drive (Shaper) and finishing (Implementer, Completer Finisher). We have no Resource Investigator and no Team Worker, so nobody naturally looks outside the team or notices tension. We compensate with two rules: Iñigo asks the client and the instructors whenever a question is open for more than a day, and Claude asks at every meeting whether anyone is stuck.

## 3. Objectives

- Hand in a specification on 17 November that Unai has reviewed and that sprint 2 can build from.
- Keep every decision in an issue, so that anyone can see why we did something.
- Finish sprint 1 with every member having written at least one document and reviewed another.

## 4. How we work

| | |
|---|---|
| **Meetings** | In class on Tuesdays and Fridays. A 15-minute check online on Thursdays at 18:00. |
| **Communication** | Work goes in issues and pull requests. Logistics in the team chat. Answer within 24 hours on working days. |
| **Availability** | Iñigo 8 h per week, Claude 8 h, Codex 6 h. Absences are announced in the chat the day before. |
| **Decisions** | Scope and order of the backlog: the Product Owner, after hearing the team. Technical decisions: by consensus, and the Scrum Master decides if there is none. The decision is written in the issue. |
| **Tasks** | Everyone signs up for tasks on the board. At most two cards in progress per person. |
| **AI assistants** | We use them. Each member reviews what they commit and can explain it. Two of us are language models; the rule applies to them too. |

## 5. Rules

1. Nobody commits to `main` directly. Everything goes through the `Sprint1` branch and a pull request.
2. A card moves to Done only when the definition of done in the sprint plan is met.
3. What the client says is information. What we write in the meeting log is what counts.
4. Keys, data files and personal data never enter the repository.
5. We do not surprise each other at the review: problems are said on Thursday at the latest.

## 6. Conflicts

| Conflict we expect | How we prevent it | What we do if it happens |
|---|---|---|
| One member does most of the work | Hours per member in the sprint plan; cards have one owner | The Scrum Master reassigns cards at the next meeting |
| Disagreement on what the client meant | Confirmed requirements have an id and a source meeting | We ask the client again before 17 November, or the Product Owner decides and records it |
| A member stops answering | Thursday check; 24-hour rule | After two missed checks the team tells the instructors |

If we cannot solve a conflict ourselves, we ask the instructors to mediate. A member who leaves or joins is announced to the instructors the same day.
